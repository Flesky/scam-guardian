# Official brand domains: Philippines

Reviewed **2026-10-09** using web search and fetches of organization websites and official government sources. Each table records the evidence for the corresponding entry in `data/brands.json`. Notes are paraphrases; no source passages are quoted.

## Method and scope

- The inventory covers the requested organizations and additional major Philippine services. Brand/product entries such as Maya Bank and Maya, or GrabPay and Grab, are distinct as requested; shared official domains may therefore appear in multiple entries.
- Domains are lowercase registrable domains (eTLD+1), not URLs or subdomains. For example, `online.bdo.com.ph` collapses to `bdo.com.ph`. The tables cover verified main sites, transaction and app portals, rewards/promotions, branded shorteners, and relevant corporate/contact domains discovered during this review. They do not claim an exhaustive worldwide inventory of every regional, infrastructure, defensive or undisclosed domain.
- Inclusion requires an organization-owned page, an explicit official link/contact address, or an official government directory/advisory. Authorized service domains such as PSA certificate partners are identified as such; authorization does not imply domain ownership. A redirect, TLS certificate, third-party list or brand-like spelling alone was not sufficient.
- Fetches were attempted. Where a page blocked access, returned only a JavaScript shell or failed, the table explicitly identifies reliance on indexed text from that official URL. That verifies the documented association, not present availability. Legacy domains and dated promotion evidence are also identified; a historical promotion is not a current offer.
- `aliases` are lowercase recognition terms. `contextWords` is empty unless an alias has an ordinary-word meaning (including Filipino words, geographic names and demonyms), such as smart, globe, maya, grab, bayad or cebuana. Context words are recognition hints, not an authentication test.
- Domain association is not a blanket safety verdict. Public profiles, payment handles, user content and redirects on official platforms can be controlled by third parties. In particular, a shortened URL needs its destination checked. Shared vendor roots (for example `bit.ly`, `onelink.me`, app stores and social networks) are not assigned to a Philippine brand merely because it publishes one link there.
